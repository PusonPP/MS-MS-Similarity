MS/MS spectra and molecular structure

Puzheng Wang | BIOF 501

Question: Do similar MS/MS spectra imply similar molecular structures?

Data and quality control

MassBank 2026.03, University of Athens. 65 raw records; 58 with shared conditions; 38 unique structures; 703 pairs.

Sample: first 65 sorted Athens_Univ filenames with exact collision energy 20 eV. This is a pilot sample, not a random sample of MassBank. An initial 10 eV pilot left only nine valid molecules; the design was changed to 20 eV before the final comparison. The proposed 61 / 48 counts were not assumed.

Shared conditions: Bruker maXis Impact, LC-ESI-QTOF, MS2, POSITIVE, [M+H]+, 20 eV. Retain m/z >= 10.0 and < precursor minus 1.5 Da. Normalize remaining peaks to their maximum; keep intensity >= 1.0% and at least 5 peaks.

Workflow

PREPROCESS_SPECTRA → SCORE_SPECTRA → COMPARE_STRUCTURES → BUILD_REPORT

matchms: greedy one-to-one matching, tolerance 0.02 Da, intensity power 1.0, m/z power 0.0. Modified cosine also allows the precursor mass shift. RDKit: Morgan radius 2, 2048 bits, chirality False; Tanimoto.

Overall results

Cosine: Pearson r = 0.446; Spearman rho = 0.086. Modified cosine: r = 0.679; rho = 0.222. Modified cosine raised 222 of 703 scores and lowered 0. Mean change: 0.044.

High spectrum / low structure (score >= 0.7, Tanimoto <= 0.3): Cosine 0; Modified cosine 6. Low spectrum / high structure (score <= 0.3, Tanimoto >= 0.7): Cosine 1; Modified cosine 0.

These cutoffs describe examples; they are not validated identification rules. Cases below use the largest modified gain and then the largest modified-score minus Tanimoto gap, excluding the first pair.

Case 1: Ciprofloxacin / Norfloxacin

Ciprofloxacin / Norfloxacin: Cosine 0.000, Modified cosine 0.976, Tanimoto 0.673. Matched peaks: 0 direct; 5 modified. A strong shifted candidate is m/z 314.1286 versus 302.1291. Their precursor losses are 18.0119 and 18.0114 Da. Similar losses can raise the modified score even when whole structures differ. Peak matches describe mass patterns; they do not prove a shared fragment structure.

Limits and interpretation

This is a small convenience sample at 20 eV. Molecules occur in many pairs, so pair scores are not independent. Correlations are descriptive; no p-values or causal claims are used. Morgan fingerprints describe 2D substructures, not full chemical identity. Chirality is ignored. Results depend on peak filters, tolerance, collision energy, fingerprint settings, and greedy matching. A higher spectral score alone cannot prove a similar structure.
